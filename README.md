## Research Projects

- [WildDepth](https://yunshin.github.io/WildDepth/): Accepted at ECCV 2026 and the CV4Animals Workshop at CVPR 2026; a public multimodal RGB-LiDAR dataset for wildlife depth estimation and 3D perception.
- [WildPose](https://denden047.github.io/wildpose.html): A long-range 3D wildlife motion capture system published as a 2025 cover article in *Journal of Experimental Biology*. [Code](https://github.com/DenDen047/WildPose_v1.1)

## Games

- [Mini Smash Bros](https://denden047.github.io/games/smash-browser/): A browser-based 2D fighting game playable with two players on the same keyboard.
- [Mini Smash (Mobile)](https://denden047.github.io/games/smash-browser-mobile/): A touch-friendly single-player version of Mini Smash — fight a CPU with simplified on-screen controls on phones and tablets.
- [Mini Terraria](https://denden047.github.io/games/terraria-like/): A browser-based 2D sandbox where you dig, gather, and build blocks.
- [Two-Player Mario-like Adventure](https://denden047.github.io/games/mario-coop/): A browser-based 2-player co-op platformer inspired by classic Mario.
- [Nyanko Wars](https://denden047.github.io/games/cat-wars/): A browser-based lane-based tower defense where you summon units to crush the enemy base.
- [Ragdoll Rumble](https://denden047.github.io/games/ragdoll-rumble/): A browser-based physics brawler where you control a wobbly active-ragdoll fighter and punch and kick enemy ragdolls across waves.
- [Machigurashi (Lagoon Life)](https://denden047.github.io/games/machigurashi/): A browser-based top-down life-sim prototype where you live, work, shop, and join events through days and seasons in a procedurally generated lagoon town.
- [Street Fight](https://denden047.github.io/games/street-fighter/): A browser-based 2D fighter with easy one-button specials (fireball, dragon punch, hurricane kick), high/low/overhead guards, throws, a super meter, and best-of-3 rounds — vs CPU (3 difficulties) or 2-player local.
- [Mythic Realm 2D](https://denden047.github.io/games/mythic-realm/): A top-down sword-and-sorcery co-op action game where a hero party fights back a monster horde — pick one of nine classes (swordsman, mage, hunter, priest, adventurer, beast tamer, dark sorcerer who summons monsters while holding down the attack, dragoon, stone idol), smash the demon gate to stop the reinforcements, then take down the stage boss in the ancient ruins or the dark forest. Beside those chapter battles there is a whole open world: twelve lands joined edge to edge, walked out from a frontier village whose altar is the only place to heal and resupply, with terrain and treasure chests that stay exactly as you left them, a map that fills in only where you have been, life vessels that raise your maximum HP, and two emblems — one from the fire dragon under the ruined temple, one from the wolf king in the everdark forest — that unseal the demon lord's throne. Falling in the field just wakes you back at the village with everything you gathered. Play solo with CPU allies (3 difficulties) or online with friends via a shared room code (serverless P2P).
- [Warzone 2D](https://denden047.github.io/games/war-zone/): A top-down 2D war shooter with RPG progression — kill to gain XP and level up your max HP and damage, with name tags and health bars over every soldier. Between battles you walk your own base on foot and step into its buildings: a supply shop for stat upgrades, weapons, parts, paint and arrows; a workbench where attachments (optic, barrel, magazine, underbarrel) move freely between guns and every weapon can be repainted — a suppressor silences your shots, a radar optic paints enemies through walls, an underbarrel SMG fires a free extra round; a forge that turns scrap taken from kills into three tiers of helmet, body and leg armour; a recruit gacha of thirteen soldiers where a duplicate raises that character's proficiency instead; and a formation tent for choosing who deploys with which three weapons. A military bow and a crossbow shoot silently from a quiver of standard, piercing or explosive arrows. Fight across a training range, an open battlefield that cycles from dawn to night, a time forest whose sword-in-the-stone slows incoming bullets, and a pitch-black jungle where something hunts anyone who runs — solo against CPU squads (3 difficulties) or against friends online via a shared room code (serverless P2P).
- [Hollow Toys](https://denden047.github.io/games/hollow-toys/): A top-down 2D survival horror set in a shuttered family pizzeria, built on real-time raycast lighting — your flashlight is cut off by walls, so what waits around the corner is genuinely invisible. Pick one of seven characters and survive three chapters and a final boss while juggling battery, sanity, breathing and per-limb injuries. Two of them wear animatronic suits: Springtrap, the yellow rabbit, and GoldBear, the golden bear — they never swing a weapon, they click on an animatronic to scare it into running away. Prowl the show stage, pirate cove, kitchen, arcade, party rooms, prize corner, ball pit, backstage and parts & service, all littered with crayon scrawls, lost shoes and birthday candles the missing children left behind. Flee a night-guard bear you cannot kill, run the Marionette's three errands, outrun the torn-apart Mangled, then break MOTHER open in the boiler room. Hide in lockers and restroom stalls, hold your breath, climb inside an empty suit to pass as one of them, and swing across rooms with the GrabPack.
- [Gacha Strikers](https://denden047.github.io/games/gacha-strikers/): A 2D arcade soccer game built around a gacha roster — win stages to earn tickets, spend them at the gacha shop in the walkable clubhouse garden, then build your own formation from 36 collectable players with elemental affinities and cut-in special moves (super shots, dribbles, blocks and keeper catches) across a 12-stage campaign.
- [Warzone: Chronofront](https://denden047.github.io/games/war-zone-pixel/): A pixel-art top-down shooter set in 1947, where a shattered "chronocore" has folded several eras into one battlefield and four armies fight over the fracture — destroy the other three bases as a rifleman, a melee-only shipwrecked ronin, a sapper who lays hidden mines and barbed wire, or a rocket-toting heavy gunner. Level up through kills, spend prize money on permanent upgrades and eight skins, crew tanks and machine-gun nests, and fight across four stages: a training yard, a ruined front with a day-night cycle, a time forest whose sword-in-the-stone slows incoming bullets, and a pitch-black jungle where something hunts anyone who runs. Solo against CPU squads (3 difficulties) or online via a shared room code (serverless P2P).
- [Forge & Crown](https://denden047.github.io/games/forge-and-crown/): A western-fantasy grand strategy RPG in the vein of *Nobunaga's Ambition*. Rise from a nameless levy to archduke across sixteen provinces of Eldenmark: lay out your castle tile by tile on terrain that changes what each building yields, run the farms, mines and taxes month by month, forge your own armour by fitting polyomino pieces into a 4×4 grid — the ores you choose decide its defence, weight, purity bonus and special move, and it carries up to three decorations — then take the field yourself in real-time top-down combat and cut down the enemy captain to earn the valour that promotes you.
- [Steel Serpent](https://denden047.github.io/games/steel-serpent/): A side-scrolling tactical espionage action game built around one exchange: dodge, then punish. Roll through an incoming bullet and the invincibility frames turn it into a *perfect dodge* — time stretches, the shooter is branded, and a RUSH stock fills. Spend it and the protagonist holsters every gun to close in with the dagger alone, chaining slashes from one branded target to the next. Outside of RUSH the seven-weapon loadout plays straight: suppressed pistol, tranquilliser, assault rifle, shotgun, anti-materiel rifle and rocket launcher, plus CQC takedowns, vision cones, alert phases and a cardboard box to hide under. Board a hijacked ferry in the rain, fight forward through a transport plane at ten thousand metres, then break into the enemy command base — four bosses, from a shotgun-swinging bruiser to a bipedal walker whose cockpit only opens after it stomps.
- [Parkour Blade](https://denden047.github.io/games/parkour-blade/): A top-down 2D parkour runner set in a night factory, where height is a real axis — a shadow and a thin tether line show how high you are, and every hazard is sorted by the height it sweeps. Orange low blades must be jumped, blue hanging blades must be slid under, red full-height blades can only be gone around, and touching any of them kills instantly. Where the floor drops away entirely, you cross by wall-kicking: cling to a steel bank in mid-air, spend grip, and kick off toward the next one before your hands give out. Six timed stages — a loading deck, a wall-kick canyon, a sawmill line, a moving-bridge shaft climbed by kicking between opposite walls, a launch-pad bay whose springboards clear the high fences, and a final corridor with all of it at once — each with checkpoints, three hidden bolts and an S-to-C rank on your best time.
